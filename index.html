Privacy Policy for HisabGo

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Welcome to HisabGo ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this Privacy Policy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the HisabGo mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect (and Don't Collect)

HisabGo is designed as a privacy-first, local application. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect, transmit, or store your personal identity information or calculation history on any of our external servers.

Local Data Storage & Android Backups

All your calculations, history, and app settings (like theme, sound, and vibration preferences) are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage using a local database.

Important Note on Data Deletion & Backups: If you clear the App's data or uninstall HisabGo, your local history will be deleted from the device. However, if you have the standard Android Auto Backup feature enabled on your smartphone, a secure copy of your App data may be backed up to your personal Google Drive account by the Android Operating System. If you reinstall the App using the same Google Account, your history and settings may be automatically restored. You can manage, disable, or delete these backups at any time directly through your Android device settings or Google Drive account.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Collection

While the core App runs offline and locally, we use specific trusted third-party services from Google to provide advertising and premium features. These services may collect certain data directly as described below:

A. Advertising (Google AdMob)

For users on the free tier, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a mobile advertising platform provided by Google LLC. To provide relevant ads and analyze ad performance, Google AdMob automatically collects certain information from your device. This includes:

  • Identifiers: IP address, Android Advertising ID (which you can reset or opt-out of via your device settings), and other unique device or account identifiers.
  • Device & Network Information: Device type, operating system version, and network connection details.
  • Usage & Diagnostic Data: Your interactions with the App and ads, diagnostic logs, and advertising/fraud-prevention related information.

This data is processed directly by Google and its third-party partners. For more detailed information on how Google uses this data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

B. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

We offer a Premium version of HisabGo to remove ads. All purchases and subscriptions are securely processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, see, or store your credit card details, billing address, or financial information. Google manages all payment processing and provides us only with the purchase status (e.g., whether you are a Premium member). Refer to the Google Play Terms of Service for more details.

3. Permissions Required by the App

HisabGo may request the following permissions on your device to function correctly:

  • Vibration Control: Used strictly for haptic feedback when pressing keypad buttons (this can be disabled in the App settings).
  • Internet Access: Required solely to fetch advertisements (via AdMob) and to verify Premium subscription status (via Google Play Billing).

4. Children's Privacy

HisabGo is a general audience application and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
