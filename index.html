Privacy Policy

WebKyra ("we", "us", or "our") operates the HisabGo Calculator mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use HisabGo, how that information is handled, and the choices available to you.

In simple terms: HisabGo is an ad-free shop and daily utility calculator designed so that all your calculation history, grocery conversions, and custom preferences remain 100% locally on your device. The App does not collect personal identifiers, and uses Google Play services solely for optional in-app purchases and update verification.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

HisabGo stores information locally on your device to deliver its features. This includes:

  • Calculation history with exact timestamps (stored on-device via Room Database)
  • Grocery conversion history and price-per-quantity records
  • User interface settings (theme mode, keypad height, button sounds, haptic vibration duration, auto-clear duration)
  • Installation timestamp used locally to manage your trial and active history retention settings

All of this data is stored in your device's internal storage sandbox. HisabGo does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details.

2. Information We Do Not Collect on External Servers

WebKyra does not operate remote servers to collect, upload, or sync your calculation entries, financial numbers, or customer notes.

We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, contact lists, or GPS location data.

3. Third-Party Services

HisabGo is built to be clean, fast, and privacy-first. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or data broker trackers:

No Advertisements & No Ad-Tracking

HisabGo is completely ad-free. The App does not use Google AdMob or any other advertising network, and does not access or track your Google Advertising ID (AAID).

Google Play Billing

HisabGo offers optional Pro upgrades (such as Monthly Starter, Yearly Pro, or Lifetime VIP) handled exclusively through Google Play Billing.

All financial transactions and payment details are processed directly and securely by Google Play. WebKyra never accesses, collects, or stores your credit card, debit card, or bank credentials.

More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service .

Google Play In-App Updates and System Diagnostics

Android and Google Play services may check for application updates and provide crash diagnostics to ensure stability and security. Any diagnostic processing is governed by Google's privacy policies.

4. Internet Access

HisabGo performs all math, calculations, and conversions entirely offline. Internet connectivity is used solely for:

  • Verifying and processing optional Premium purchases through Google Play Billing
  • Checking for application updates via the Google Play In-App Update API
  • Opening external links when explicitly requested by you (such as this Privacy Policy or support links)

5. Device Permissions

HisabGo requests only the minimal permissions necessary for its functionality:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely for optional haptic feedback when pressing keypad buttons. This can be customized or turned off in Settings.
  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used strictly for Google Play in-app purchases, app updates, and legal/support links.

HisabGo does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, storage files, contacts, or location.

6. How We Use Information

Data processed locally on your device or via Google Play services is used exclusively to:

  • Perform calculations and grocery conversions
  • Retain calculation history for your chosen retention period (active calculation days)
  • Remember your interface and sound preferences
  • Verify and restore optional Premium purchases
  • Maintain application stability and update compatibility

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Because your calculations remain on your local device, no calculation history is shared with any third party.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You have complete control over all calculation records:

  • Manual Deletion: You can delete any individual entry or wipe your entire history at any time using the "Clear All History" feature.
  • Auto-Retention: You can configure how many active calculation days your history is kept (default 2 active days for standard free access; up to 15 active days for Pro/Trial).
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application or clearing app data through Android Settings permanently removes all locally stored calculations and preferences.

9. Security

Your calculations and preferences are stored within Android's sandboxed private app storage. We do not transmit your store calculations over external networks, ensuring your data remains completely private to your phone.

10. Children's Privacy

HisabGo is a general-purpose utility calculator and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You can control keypad sounds, haptic duration, auto-clear timer, themes, and history retention directly inside the App's Settings screen.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
